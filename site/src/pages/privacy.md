## Hosting

The site is served by Firebase Hosting, a Google service. To deliver pages, keep the service secure
and give us traffic statistics, Google's servers process your IP address, the address of the page you
ask for, your browser's user agent and the time. This is needed to show you the site at all. Google
keeps these request logs for a few months, then deletes them. Our legal basis is our legitimate
interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google acts as our processor under its data
processing terms.

## Analytics (only with your consent)

If you click _Accept_ in the cookie banner, we load Google Analytics 4 (measurement id
`{{MEASUREMENT_ID}}`), provided by Google. It tells us how many people visit and which pages they find
useful.

- **What is collected:** the pages you view and when, the page you came from, how far you scroll,
  links you follow to other sites, files you download and searches on the site, your device type,
  operating system, browser, screen size and language, and an approximate location (country and city)
  worked out from your IP address. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses.
- **Cookies:** `_ga` and `_ga_<id>` hold a random identifier so repeat visits count once. They are set
  for this site only and expire 13 months after you accept; later visits don't extend them.
- **What we have turned off:** Google signals, advertising features and ad personalisation.
- **How long it is kept:** data about visitors and their visits is kept for 2 months, then deleted.
  Aggregated reports with no link to any visitor (such as visits per month) are kept.
- **Legal basis:** your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive for the
  cookies).
- **It is voluntary:** declining has no effect on how the site works.

**Changing your mind:** use _Cookie settings_ at the bottom of any page. Declining there stops
analytics straight away and deletes the analytics cookies. If your browser sends a
[Global Privacy Control](https://globalprivacycontrol.org/) signal, we treat it as declining and don't
ask; only an _Accept_ you give in _Cookie settings_ while the signal is on overrides it.
